# Daily Spend: main screen

Scope: `index.html`, the whole app (one screen). Visitor mode: Operate.

Audience and job: anyone logging what they spent today, usually on a phone, in a few seconds. Logging today leads; the panda sits close by. A polished result would feel wrong if it were too serious or too plain.

Chosen direction: Loyalty Stamp Card, Pastel Mix palette (user-picked after two re-rolls and two colour steers). Memorable moment: the stamp thumping onto today's circle as the panda eats.

Unresolved: none.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Logging a day earns a rubber stamp on the month's loyalty card. Refuses the fintech dashboard of grey tiles with one green accent, and refuses the heatmap-style streak grid.

OWN-WORLD: Blush ground (#FBF3F7), white cards outlined in 2px plum ink (#3D3350), pill controls with the same outline, Rubik in 500/700/900. Stamps are pastel discs with an inner ink ring, slightly rotated; each day's stamp takes the pastel of its largest category. Unstamped days are dashed circles.

STORY: The visitor sees where to type an amount, logs it, watches a stamp land and the panda eat, and understands that an unbroken run of stamps earns the panda gifts.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header with name and panda mark. One white label: panda with mood line and five-stamp tummy on top, then the amount field as the largest element on screen, category pills, and the lilac "Log and feed" pill. The month's stamp card begins directly below.

FORM: Loyalty stamp card; user-pinned from the round-two hand with palette steers "i like loyalty stamp card but i dont like the colours" and "make it pastel", final pick "Pastel Mix". seed:85e367d5fc54cf1e
